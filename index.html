From the report

Dutch & Dutch 8c pair on 914 mm stands; 20° toe-in; six corner-trap modules in three stacks; two 120 × 60 cm ceiling panels; two 180 × 60 cm side diffusers; studio desk and DJ desk with two SL-1200MK2 decks and a DJM-450.

Visual assumptions

Window sizes and height, exact door dimensions, oak flooring, wall paint, lighting, chair design and desk accessories are interpretations. Desk height is assumed to be 780 mm. QRD well pattern is illustrative.

Preview states. Treatment starts on to show the intended finished room; the cloud and diffusers were unmounted at the report date. The proposed arrangement uses 1.72 m spacing, a 1.95 m listening position and a 100 mm desk shift toward the rear. Cloud centres are recalculated with the image-source method; diffuser placement stays as reported.

A spatial visualization, not an acoustic simulation. No performance predictions are inferred from the front-wall window correction. Standard WebGL graphics, rendered locally with Three.js. No external assets or account required.
